What you do in 90 minutes

Here's Wire your first circuit, from the door to the drive home. Every session follows the same shape.

  1. 0 to 10 Arrive and settle in.

    Grab a tea, get the safety brief, gloves and glasses on.

  2. 10 to 20 See the finished job.

    Your tutor shows you a working circuit and tells you how they got into the trade.

  3. 20 to 65 Your turn.

    In pairs at a bench, you wire a socket and a light on a practice board.

  4. 65 to 75 Test it.

    Flick the switch. Does the light come on?

  5. 75 to 90 Straight talk.

    What construction apprentices earn by law (€7.91 to €21.37 an hour), the qualified craft floor (about €23.74 an hour), how long training really takes, and your next step if you want one.

Fair questions before you book

Do I need experience or my own tools?

No. Come as you are. Tools and safety gear are supplied. [CONFIRM]

Is this a qualification?

No. It's a taster. You leave knowing if you want to go further and what further looks like.

How long does it take to qualify properly?

Four years on paper. Backlogs have pushed the real average closer to six, according to the Irish Examiner. You earn while you train. Better to hear that now.

Will I be the oldest, or the only woman?

Maybe. Women are about 1% of the trades today, by Property Industry Ireland's count, and we'd like that to change. Every session is built for complete beginners. [AGE POLICY]

Can I go straight into heat pumps?

Not directly. Installer courses are for qualified plumbers, electricians and refrigeration craftspeople. The taster shows you the work so you know which trade gets you there.
